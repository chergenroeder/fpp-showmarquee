    How it works
    • The pages above take turns on the matrix in this order, each held still for Seconds per page. Each message is a page of its own. Social media sites with the same handle share one page; a different handle gets a page of its own.
    • A page with nothing to show right now is skipped until it has: the countdown on the day itself, the weather with no reading, a message outside its dates.
    • Nothing changes on the matrix until you Save. The preview on the right plays the rotation as the matrix will.
    • The Number style and Text style on the Display tab colour every page. A dot on a tab means that page needs something before it can show.

    No logo
    Off: the logo shows on its own, as large as the matrix allows.
    PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP or SVG. It's saved on the player as soon as you upload it (no need to Save), shrunk to at most 512 pixels, and its transparent or black edges are trimmed so it fills the matrix. Transparent parts show as black. It isn't part of Download settings: on a new player, upload it again.
    The name is drawn as large as fits, in the Text style's font. With a logo, the name goes beside it, or under it when the logo is wide. Colourful, bright logos with bold shapes look best on LEDs: check the preview.

    Choose where the weather comes from: nothing is shown or sent until you do.

    More wind, symbol and source options
    Open-Meteo readings are hidden once 45 minutes old (three missed updates).
    The symbol is the first that applies: a storm, snow, sleet, rain, fog, the wind when it's windy (above), clouds, then the sun by day and the moon at night. Open-Meteo says whether it's day at your location; a Tempest goes by its light sensor; until either says, 7:00–19:00 on this player's clock is day. The temperature uses the Number style, wind and gusts the Text style.
    Social Media
    Logos on top, @handle below, held still (never scrolled). Sites with the same handle share one screen; different handles get a screen each, shown one after another. Each screen is a page of its own, shown for the seconds per page.
    The handle uses the Text style's font and colour, shrinking if it's too wide. Logos are trademarks of their owners.
    Show Prep
    Tells viewers to get your show's audio app before the show: a QR code they scan with their phone's camera, beside your message.
    The app viewers should get
    Choose the app: the page doesn't show until you do.
    The app's name uses the Number style, the rest of the message the Text style. Shorter links make smaller codes, which can be drawn with more LEDs per square (easier to scan).
